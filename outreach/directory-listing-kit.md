Regles respectees: pas de langage courtage (宅建), pas de prix, pas de nom de source data, pas de tiret long.

## Champs de base
- Site name / Blog title: **Tokyo Expat**
- URL: **https://www.tokyo-expat.com**
- Country from: **France**
- Country in / Living in: **Japan**
- City: **Tokyo**
- Language: English & French
- Category: Asia > Japan (Relocation / Living abroad / Housing)

## Description courte (~35 mots)
Tokyo Expat is a bilingual guide (English and French) for foreigners moving to Tokyo. Written by a foreign resident living in the city, it covers neighborhoods, housing, rents, and the practical side of settling into Japan.

## Description longue (~75 mots)
Tokyo Expat helps foreigners settle in Tokyo. Run by a foreign resident who lives and operates furnished rentals in the city, the site publishes practical, bilingual guides (English and French) on choosing a neighborhood, understanding Japanese rental rules, typical rents by ward and station, and the everyday realities of moving to Japan. It also offers hands-on relocation and housing support for newcomers who want a guide through the process.
