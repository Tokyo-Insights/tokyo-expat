## Strategie de liens (demande d'Alessandro du 13/09)

Probleme : tokyo-expat.com n'a **aucune** page Fukuoka. Le seul actif data est l'indice
**Tokyo** `/en/data`. Lier une page Fukuoka a un indice Tokyo sans raison serait un
lien parachute, que l'editeur retire ou que le lecteur ne clique pas.

Solution : le lien est **gagne par le texte**. La section contient un paragraphe de
comparaison avec Tokyo (utile aux familles qui arbitrent entre les deux villes), et
c'est LA que le lien vers l'indice Tokyo tombe naturellement.

Deux liens, pas plus (trois ressemblerait a du bourrage et donnerait envie de tout couper) :
1. **En cours de texte**, sur la comparaison Tokyo -> `https://www.tokyo-expat.com/en/data`
2. **Ligne de credit finale**, sur la marque -> `https://www.tokyo-expat.com`

Trois garde-fous contre l'oubli ou la perte des liens :
- La section est fournie en **Markdown ET en HTML pret a coller**. Si son CMS ne
  convertit pas le Markdown, l'ancre meurt en silence : le bloc HTML supprime ce risque.
- L'email **nomme explicitement les deux liens** et rappelle qu'ils sont suivis,
  "comme la section Tokyo". Les omettre devient un geste conscient, plus un oubli.
- La ligne de credit est **inseparable de la donnee** : elle porte le denombrement
  (12 700 annonces), donc la retirer prive le chiffre de sa source.

## What Family Housing Actually Costs Around Fukuoka's International School Areas

Once you have a school in mind, Fukuoka narrows the housing search fast. The city is
compact enough that almost every ward is commutable, so the real question is not "can we
get there" but "what does each area cost, and what are we giving up". Here is what the
rental market looks like today, ward by ward, for family-size flats.

**Chuo, the centre.** The most expensive ward, and the newest housing. A two-bedroom
(2LDK in Japanese listings) runs around 167,000 yen a month at a median 60 sqm, a
three-bedroom (3LDK) around 190,000 yen at 77 sqm. Listings sit a median seven to eight
minutes' walk from a station and the typical building is only eight years old. Families
who want to walk to everything concentrate here.

**The western wards: Sawara, Nishi, Jonan.** The seafront side of the city, and where a
lot of family housing sits. Sawara runs about 118,000 yen for a 2LDK and 140,000 for a
3LDK, Nishi about 93,000 and 112,000, Jonan about 90,000 and 114,000. You get more space
and quieter streets, in buildings typically 17 to 27 years old.

**Hakata, Higashi and Minami.** Hakata is around 130,000 yen for a 2LDK, Higashi 92,000
and 133,000, Minami 90,000 and 105,000. One number deserves a warning. Hakata's
three-bedroom median, 129,500 yen, reads as cheaper than its two-bedroom, and that is not
a bargain: the three-bedroom stock there has a median age of 21 years against 8 for
two-bedrooms. You are pricing older buildings, not a discount.

**How this compares with Tokyo.** A three-bedroom in Fukuoka's dearest ward costs less
than a two-bedroom in Setagaya, a mid-market Tokyo ward where the median is around
230,000 yen for roughly the same floor area. Families weighing one city against the other
can check that directly in our
[ward-by-ward rent index for Tokyo](https://www.tokyo-expat.com/en/data).

**The trade-off families underestimate.** Three-bedroom supply is thin and older
everywhere except Chuo. Across the city there are roughly two two-bedroom listings for
every three-bedroom one, so a family that needs three bedrooms in a newer building is
really choosing between Chuo prices and an older building elsewhere. Decide which of the
two you are willing to bend on before you start viewing, because the market will not
offer you both in most wards.

A practical order of operations: fix the school, let it narrow the wards, then compare
rents inside that shortlist. Doing it the other way round is how families end up moving
twice.

*Rent figures are medians from roughly 12,700 active family-size rental listings across
Fukuoka's seven wards, compiled by [Tokyo Expat](https://www.tokyo-expat.com), which
publishes rent data for Japanese cities.*
